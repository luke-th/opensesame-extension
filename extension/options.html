OpenSesame

Hold your shortcut and click a link to open it in a new window on your other screen.

Shortcut

Click Change, then hold the modifier keys and, optionally, one regular key (e.g. ⌃⌥, ⌘Q, or just `). Release to save, Esc to cancel. Modifiers must match exactly when you click.

A regular key is only seen once the page has focus, so click the page first if you've just used the address bar. Browser shortcuts like ⌘W still fire while recording, so avoid those.
